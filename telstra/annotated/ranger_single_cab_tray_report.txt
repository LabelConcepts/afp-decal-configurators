METHOD NOTE ON D1 vs P1 (driver vs passenger side identification)
Both side-on photos show a large blue/red Telstra T decal on the front door, so the two sides could not
be told apart by the decal alone. Independently verified via the Ford Ranger keyless-entry SecuriCode
keypad, which Ford fits ONLY to the driver's door, on the B-pillar edge behind the front door glass:
 - The photo showing this black raised-button keypad on the B-pillar = DRIVER side = D1.
 - The photo showing a plain door handle with NO keypad in that position = PASSENGER side = P1.
This was confirmed independently on both p2 and p3 (same physical keypad visible/absent in the
corresponding photos on each page), and cross-checked against the rear photo (RHD AU vehicle: standing
behind the vehicle, the driver's side appears on the right of the rear photo, matching the side where
the keypad-equipped door photo was taken from).
