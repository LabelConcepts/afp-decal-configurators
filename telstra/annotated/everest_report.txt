EVEREST (Ford Everest 2023 - Supergraphic Livery) - Annotation Report
======================================================================
Source PDF pages used: everest_p2.png (PARTS ON VEHICLE), everest_p3.png (INSTALLED PHOTOS
+ REAR UPDATE: MAY-JUNE 2024 mockup). Image size for both: 1521 x 1075 px.

Method: every coordinate below was measured directly from the source PNG using Python/PIL
crops zoomed in at 3-4x, and for D1/P1 cross-checked with an objective color-based centroid
(HSV/RGB threshold on the Telstra blue) rather than eyeballed. Each placement was re-rendered
with the actual draw_tag() call and visually re-inspected before being accepted (see
annotated/everest_p2_tagged.png and annotated/everest_p3_tagged.png).

PLACED TAGS
-----------

D1 - Drive rear side Telstra T decal (539mm high)
  Page: everest_p2.png  ->  annotated/everest_p2_tagged.png
  Pixel coords: (426, 303)  [center of the blue "T" mark, TOP-LEFT side-view photo]
  Verification: isolated blue pixels in the photo region (R<G<B, B>140, B>R+40) and took the
  centroid -> (426.4, 302.8), matched visually to the T stroke.

P1 - Pass rear side Telstra T decal (539mm high)
  Page: everest_p2.png  ->  annotated/everest_p2_tagged.png
  Pixel coords: (621, 709)  [center of the blue "T" mark, BOTTOM-LEFT side-view photo]
  Verification: same blue-pixel centroid method -> (621.2, 709.3).

  IMPORTANT CAVEAT on D1 vs P1 assignment: the fitting-guide photos do not caption which
  side-view photo is literally the driver's side vs. passenger's side. I confirmed the two
  photos are genuinely two different sides (not a mirrored duplicate) — the top photo's rear
  quarter panel has no fuel filler flap visible, while the bottom photo's rear quarter panel
  clearly shows a fuel filler flap outline — so they are not the same physical panel. However
  I could not positively identify from the images alone which side is drive vs. pass (no
  visible steering wheel, badge, or captioned reference). I assigned D1 to the TOP photo and
  P1 to the BOTTOM photo purely by reading-order convention (matching the Page 1 checklist's
  "DRIVE ... / PASS ..." left-to-right listing and top-to-bottom photo order). If the client
  knows which physical side was photographed first, this label pair (D1/P1) may need to be
  swapped — the pixel positions themselves are correct for "a side T-decal," only the D/P
  naming is a best-effort assumption.

SKIPPED

W1 - Internal windscreen 'Parking Brake' decal: SKIPPED - unverifiable.
  Checked the only front-on windscreen view available (everest_p2.png, top-right quadrant,
  front grille photo). There are two/three small stickers visible in the upper-right corner
  of the windscreen (driver's side top corner), but at full zoom they remain too low-resolution
  to read any text — they look consistent with standard compliance/registration stickers
  (one appears to carry a vehicle-rego-style green/colour-coded sticker), not a readable
  "Parking Brake" decal. No other page/photo shows the windscreen close enough to confirm.
  Per instructions, not tagging a position I cannot actually read.

W2 - Internal windscreen 'GPS' decal: SKIPPED - unverifiable, same reason as W1. Same
  windscreen corner was inspected at 4x zoom; no legible "GPS" decal could be confirmed.
