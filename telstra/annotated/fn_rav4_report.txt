MODEL-YEAR / BODY NOTE
This vehicle's own photos (front view, p2 top-right) show an older-style RAV4 front end (narrower LED
daytime-running strip, different grille/bumper texture, different plate "1PU 5BD") compared to the
base `rav4` document's "2026 MODEL" refresh (different grille/headlight shape, plate "2EW 4GN"). These
are confirmed to be two different photo sets of two different model years, NOT the same vehicle body.
Fortunately, fn_rav4's own 4 pages independently show every item on its checklist except the two
internal windscreen decals (see SKIPPED section) — no cross-referencing against the `rav4` document was
actually needed for D1/P1/T1-T5, contrary to the initial assumption that cross-referencing might be
required. This avoids mixing model years into one vehicle's artwork, per the stated methodology risk.

METHOD NOTE ON D2/D3 vs P2/P3 (which mandala is the "Extended" design)
The checklist text itself states the asymmetry explicitly (not inferred): driver side has the "Extended"
variant at the REAR QTR (D3) with a plain "First Nations" decal at the DOOR (D2); passenger side has the
"Extended" variant at the DOOR (P2) with a plain "First Nations" decal at the REAR QTR (P3). This was
cross-checked visually: the two "Extended" motifs (D3, P2) both show an added outer ring of small linked
circles/beads around the core mandala, while the plain motifs (D2, P3) show a simpler toothed/gear-ring
border with no linked-circle embellishment — confirming the checklist's own left/right labelling matches
what's actually drawn on each side, rather than assuming symmetry.
