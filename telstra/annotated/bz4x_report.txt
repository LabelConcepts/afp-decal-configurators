Methodology note on D1/P1 (driver vs passenger) determination:
bz4x_p2.png contains 4 photos: top-left (side view), top-right (front view, AU plate
"2AD*8WF" visible), bottom-left (side view), bottom-right (rear view, matching plate +
rear decals). Car is RHD (Australian plate), so driver's side = vehicle's right-hand
side when facing the direction of travel.
- Top-left photo: rear taillight visible at the image's LEFT edge, front fender/headlight
  at the image's RIGHT edge (front-at-right). By compass reasoning (front-at-right in a
  broadside photo places the camera on the vehicle's right/driver side), this is the
  DRIVER side => used for D1.
- Bottom-left photo: front fender/headlight visible at the image's LEFT edge, rear
  taillight at the RIGHT edge (front-at-left) => camera on the vehicle's left/passenger
  side => used for P1.
Each side was independently inspected (zoomed crops of both front fenders) rather than
assumed as a mirror of the other - confirmed genuinely different crops/backgrounds/angle,
not a duplicated image.
