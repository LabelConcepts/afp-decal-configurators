fn_hiace_lwb — First Nations - Toyota Hiace LWB
Annotation report
=========================================================

Source pages: p1 = checklist (not used for tagging). p2 = full vehicle photo set
(2x2 grid: top-left driver profile, bottom-left passenger profile, top-right front 3/4,
bottom-right rear elevation — same layout convention as hiace_supergraphic, and in fact
page 2 of this document is explicitly titled "DRIVERS SIDE ONLY" for the Connection decal
callout at top, confirming top=driver). p3 = close-up photo explicitly labelled "DRIVERS
SIDE 'FIRST NATIONS' DECALS". p4 = close-up photo explicitly labelled "PASSENGER SIDE
'FIRST NATIONS' DECALS". These explicit labels on p3/p4 remove any doubt about which side
is which (no guessing required), and they corroborate the driver=top / passenger=bottom
convention used for p2 and for hiace_supergraphic.

  annotated/fn_hiace_lwb_p2_tagged.png  (D1, P1, W1, W2, T1-T5)

--- Items on fn_hiace_lwb_p3.png (driver-side First Nations artwork close-up) ---

  D2  Drive door 'First Nations' decal (defined by the checklist as "the SMALLER of the
      two" driver-side FN artwork panels)
      Placed at pixel (1015, 715) — the smaller circular motif, positioned further aft,
      near the rear wheel arch / sliding-door area.

  D3  Drive side large 'First Nations' decal (defined by the checklist as "the LARGER" of
      the two, "likely on a side panel/toolbox")
      Placed at pixel (400, 505) — the larger circular motif, positioned further forward,
      spanning the front door / fender area.

      IMPORTANT NOTE on D2 vs D3: the checklist's own location hints ("door" for D2,
      "side panel/toolbox" for D3) do not cleanly match the geometry actually seen in the
      photo — the LARGER circle sits closer to the front door, and the SMALLER circle sits
      further back near the rear wheel/sliding-door area, i.e. the reverse of what the
      "door"/"side panel" wording alone might suggest. Since the task defines D2 and D3
      primarily by relative SIZE ("the smaller" / "the larger") and only secondarily and
      tentatively by location ("likely"), size was used as the deciding factor: D2 =
      smaller circle, D3 = larger circle, exactly as the two codes' own descriptions
      state. Flagging this explicitly so it can be double-checked against the physical
      decal set if the door/panel wording matters more than size for install purposes.

  D4  Drive 'Connection....' clear decal (driver side only, artist credit)
      Placed at pixel (1030, 927) — this is the small on-vehicle text decal itself
      ("Connection, © Bobbi Lockyer/Copyright Agency"), found by following the document's
      own magenta pointer line from the "ARTIST CREDIT DRIVE SIDE ONLY" label to its tip
      (measured directly, not guessed), which lands exactly on this small text decal
      low on the door, near the rocker panel.

--- Items on fn_hiace_lwb_p4.png (passenger-side First Nations artwork close-up) ---

  P2  Pass door 'First Nations' decal (smaller of the two passenger-side panels, mirroring
      D2's size-based rule)
      Placed at pixel (497, 702) — smaller circular motif, near the front door/fender,
      mirrored layout vs. the driver side.

  P3  Pass side large 'First Nations' decal (larger of the two)
      Placed at pixel (1145, 530) — larger circular motif, further aft.

  P4  Pass rear qtr 'First Nations' decal — NOT PLACED / UNVERIFIABLE.
      This is the one asymmetric item (driver side has no equivalent "rear qtr" code).
      I examined fn_hiace_lwb_p4.png (passenger close-up) AND the full passenger-side
      overview photo on fn_hiace_lwb_p2.png (bottom-left quadrant) AND the rear-elevation
      photo on fn_hiace_lwb_p2.png (bottom-right quadrant). In all three, only the two FN
      artwork circles already tagged as P2/P3 are visible; the body area between the large
      circle (P3) and the rear doors/tail-lights (where the big Telstra "T"/orange swoosh
      decal sits) shows no additional, separate First Nations design element, and the rear
      elevation photo shows no FN artwork at all (only the Telstra/byda/GreenFleet rear
      decals). I am therefore NOT guessing a position for P4 — it could not be confirmed in
      any of the photos available in this document. This should be checked against the
      actual physical decal set or a higher-resolution/alternate photo before fitting, in
      case it exists but simply isn't rendered in this particular artwork mockup.
