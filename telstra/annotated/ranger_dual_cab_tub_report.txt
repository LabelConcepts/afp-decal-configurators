DRIVER vs PASSENGER SIDE VERIFICATION (critical — this exact mix-up happened twice before)
Australia is right-hand-drive, so the driver's side is the vehicle's physical right side.
For a stationary vehicle photographed from each flank, the nose appears on the RIGHT of
frame when viewing the driver (right/RHD) side, and on the LEFT of frame when viewing the
passenger (left) side (verified by vector/geometry reasoning, not assumption).
Applied this to each photo:
  - p2 top-left quadrant: nose points RIGHT -> driver side -> D1.
  - p2 bottom-left quadrant: nose points LEFT -> passenger side -> P1.
  - p3 top-left photo: nose points RIGHT -> driver side -> D1 (alt angle).
  - p3 bottom-left photo: nose points LEFT -> passenger side -> P1 (alt angle).

p3 bottom-left (both identified as P1/passenger above), the orange/blue decal visibly
straddles a door handle, consistent with the cutout note. p4's measurement diagram overlays
the identical two side photos as p2 and independently confirms the same pairing (same
nose-left/right orientation per photo). D1 and P1 were confirmed to be non-mirror-image
placements — the decal sits in a different position relative to the doors/handles on each
side (visually distinct crops verified independently, not copied from one side to the
