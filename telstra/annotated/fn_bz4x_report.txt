Methodology note on D1/P1 (driver vs passenger) determination:
Same front/rear compass method as the bz4x vehicle (RHD, AU plate => driver = vehicle's
right-hand side facing travel direction), applied independently to this vehicle's own
photos:
- p2 top-left photo: rear taillight at image-LEFT, front fender at image-RIGHT =>
  DRIVER side => D1.
- p2 bottom-left photo: front fender at image-LEFT, rear taillight at image-RIGHT =>
  PASSENGER side => P1.
This was cross-checked (not merely assumed) against the two dedicated, explicitly
labelled close-up pages:
- p3 is explicitly titled "DRIVERS SIDE 'FIRST NATIONS' DECALS". Its two First Nations
  medallions have distinctive, non-symmetric inner artwork (left = spiral/gear concentric
  rings, right = ring-of-dots pattern). The SAME two distinctive patterns, in the same
  front-to-rear order, appear in p2's top-left photo - confirming top-left = driver side.
- p4 is explicitly titled "PASSENGER SIDE 'FIRST NATIONS' DECALS". Its medallions
  (left = 8-petal flower/dot pattern, right = concentric wave-ring pattern) likewise match
  p2's bottom-left photo - confirming bottom-left = passenger side.
This double confirmation (geometric + artwork-pattern cross-check) was done specifically
because this exact driver/passenger mix-up has happened before on this project.
