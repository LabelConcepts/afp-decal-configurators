For each item, the actual decal pixel position was independently measured
(not copied from the OCR/label text position) by:
  1. Isolating the cyan/blue callout-box outline color in the image via numpy
     (RGB ~ (0,174,239), distinguishing it from the darker label-line color).
  2. Finding the box's true left/right/top/bottom pixel edges from column/row
     sum peaks (avoiding contamination from the diagonal leader lines).
  3. Computing the geometric center of each box.
  4. Visually verifying every computed point by overlaying a marker dot on a
     zoomed crop and confirming it lands on the actual decal artwork, not
     just inside the box outline or on the leader line.
