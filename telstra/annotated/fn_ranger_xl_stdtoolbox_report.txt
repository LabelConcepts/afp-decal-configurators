METHODOLOGY NOTE ON REAR CLUSTER (T1-T5): the rear-view photo on this vehicle's p2 LOOKS
visually identical at a glance to ranger_xl_superg's rear photo (same canopy box, same
window, same "1MP 6JL" numberplate — almost certainly the same physical rear box/vehicle,
since the FN livery is applied to the cab doors/toolbox only, not the rear canopy).
However, per instructions I did NOT assume this and copy superg's T1-T5 pixel coordinates.
I pixel-diffed the two full source images in the shared rear-photo region (box
1090,160-1521,545) with ImageChops.difference and got a non-trivial mean difference
(~60/255, bbox covering most of the crop) — i.e. the photo placement/scale on the page is
NOT pixel-identical between the two PDFs (slightly different vertical position/crop), even
though the content is the same vehicle. Using superg's raw coordinates on this image would
have been WRONG (this is exactly the kind of mistake flagged for this project). Instead I
independently re-measured every T-code directly on fn_ranger_xl_stdtoolbox_p2.png by cropping
box (1090,280)-(1521,470), zooming 3x, visually locating each of the 5 decals, converting
zoomed coordinates back to full-image pixels, then re-cropping at the final candidate point
with a drawn crosshair and visually confirming the crosshair sits on the decal before
accepting it (see crops/fn_rear_check_final.png, crops/fn_check_d_points2.png,
crops/fn_check_p_points.png — all generated during this pass).
