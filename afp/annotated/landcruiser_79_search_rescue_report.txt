Side identification method: the front-view photo (top-right panel) shows a snorkel intake pipe
mounted on the image-left side of the windscreen. For a vehicle facing the camera, image-left
corresponds to the vehicle's own right-hand side; in Australia (RHD) the right-hand side is the
DRIVER's side. The top-left photo's near/visible flank also clearly shows this same snorkel
mounted on the A-pillar, so that photo = DRIVER side. The bottom-left photo (different, outdoor
background, no snorkel on the visible flank) is therefore the PASSENGER side by elimination. This
reasoning is noted explicitly because the two flank photos are otherwise visually symmetric and it
would be easy to mix them up.
