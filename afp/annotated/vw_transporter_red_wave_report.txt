vw_transporter_red_wave — AFP VW Transporter (LWB), 2021 WAVE design, Red vehicle, 16 items
=============================================================================================

SOURCE IMAGE NOTE: as with vw_passat_fitting, the original source_images png was
the checklist text page, not a photo. Rebuilt from the PDF's real photos
(124562 - AFP - 2021 LWB VW TRANSPORTER RED - WAVE - FITTING.pdf): the "VISUAL"
2x2 photo grid (page 2: driver side, passenger side, front, rear of an actual
red-liveried van) was used directly — it is a clean, well-lit studio shot with
no cropping needed, and a separate "REFLECTIVE PARTS" page (page 4) in the same
PDF carries pre-existing leader-line labels ("TRANS24 F1/F2/F3", "TRANS24 R1")
confirming exactly where those reflective strip codes belong — I cross-checked
every F1/F2/F3/R1 placement against that labelled diagram before tagging the
unlabelled photo.

DRIVE/PASS SIDE ASSIGNMENT CAVEAT: same as noted for vw_passat_fitting — both
side photos are mirror-symmetric in content and no asymmetric cue was visible
to independently confirm driver vs passenger. Side 1 (nose right in photo) =
DRIVE, Side 2 (nose left) = PASS, by convention, consistent with the other two
vehicles in this batch. Measured independently on each side photo regardless.

RESULT: All 16 items placed with photo verification. No items skipped.
