NOTE: the file originally saved at this path was the text PART CHECKLIST page (page 3 of the
8-page fitting PDF) with no vehicle photo at all. Replaced with page 2 ("VISUAL") of the same
source PDF (TOYOTA PRADO/GENERAL DUTIES/AFP - PRADO LANDCRUISER - TAIL GATE - KINGS CREST -
FITTING.pdf), a 2x2 grid of real installed-vehicle photos: top-left = one flank, top-right =
front, bottom-left = other flank, bottom-right = rear. Every coordinate below was independently
measured from this photo.

Side identification: unlike the LandCruiser SRG (which had a snorkel to anchor driver vs
passenger), this Prado's two flank photos show no reliable asymmetric exterior feature (no
snorkel, no visible fuel flap, no external tailgate hinge in frame) to conclusively fix which
photo is physically the driver's side vs passenger's side. I confirmed via nose direction that
the two photos are genuinely opposite flanks (top photo: nose right, tail/taillight at left edge;
bottom photo: nose left, tail at right edge) so they are not duplicates. I assigned top photo =
"FRONT" (matches the checklist's FRONT-x codes) and bottom photo = "PASS" (matches PASS-x codes)
by position/order only. FLAGGED FOR CONFIRMATION: if this assignment is backwards, every FRONT-x
and PASS-x tag below is simply on the wrong physical side of the vehicle (decal content/positions
are mirror-identical on both flanks, so nothing is missing — only the Front/Pass labelling could
be swapped).

The front guard has a pre-existing labelled reference diagram (page 4 of the same PDF, a night/UV
photo with "PRA GD F1–F5" and "PRA GD R1" leader-line labels already drawn by Label Concepts) which
I used ONLY to confirm what the "PRA" codes refer to (the 4 lime/fluoro corner accent pieces on the
bull bar/hood edge, and the lime strip on the rear bumper) — not to copy coordinates, since that
diagram is a different photo shoot with different framing. All PRA coordinates below were
independently re-measured on the actual output image (the 2x2 grid).
