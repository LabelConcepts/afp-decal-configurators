points (D1, P1, F1-F5, R1, R2) + reflective strip quantities. The PDF's own page 4

vehicle — those labels were used to confirm naming/position, then every coordinate below
was independently re-measured on the clean, well-lit photo (page 2), not copied from the
pre-existing labels' pixel positions.
