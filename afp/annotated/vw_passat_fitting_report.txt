vw_passat_fitting — AFP VW Passat (Fitting guide), 16 items
=============================================================

IMPORTANT NOTE ON SOURCE IMAGE: the original source_images/vw_passat_fitting.png
produced by the extraction pipeline was the PART-CHECKLIST TEXT PAGE of the
fitting-guide PDF (page 1 of 7) — it contains no photo of the vehicle at all, so
no decal position could be verified against it. The underlying PDF
(AFP_VW PASSAT - FITTING.pdf, at /Volumes/LabelConcepts/Workspace/AFP/WORKING
FILES/VW Passat/) actually contains a "VISUAL" page (page 2) with a 2x2 photo
grid (driver-side, passenger-side, front, rear of an actual installed/livery
vehicle) plus a dedicated full-size "INSTALL PHOTO" of the tailgate (page 4).
I rebuilt source_images/vw_passat_fitting.png from those real photos (stacked:
Side 1 photo, Side 2 photo, then the large rear photo) so every item below was
measured against an actual photo, not the checklist text. Flagging this pipeline
issue since the same bug likely affected other AFP vehicles rendered from this
PDF set.

DRIVE/PASS SIDE ASSIGNMENT CAVEAT: both side photos are mirror-symmetric in
content (same crest/phone-number/chequers layout on each), and no asymmetric
visual cue (steering wheel, fuel flap, etc.) was visible in any of the available
photos to independently confirm which physical side is "driver" vs "passenger".
I assigned Side 1 (car's nose pointing right in the photo) = DRIVE and Side 2
(nose pointing left) = PASS, by convention, consistently. Each side's decals
were still measured completely independently (no coordinates copied between
the two side photos). Recommend client confirm Drive/Pass assignment against
the physical vehicle.

RESULT: 15 of 16 items placed with real photo verification. 1 item
(ITEM-2) skipped as unverifiable — see note below.

SKIPPED (unverifiable):
  ITEM-2   "2x 2250mm @ 35mm VC612 LIME STRIPS"                 -> SKIPPED
           Same lime/yellow pinstripe family as ITEM-1. The checklist lists
           two different strip lengths (3500mm and 2250mm) under one "SIDE
           REFLECTIVE" heading, but in every available photo the pinstripe
           along each side reads as a single continuous line from the rear
           crest to the front wheel arch — no visible seam, colour change, or
           break distinguishes a second, shorter segment. I could not locate
           a position for this item that I could independently confirm is a
           physically distinct decal rather than the same line already tagged
           as ITEM-1. Reporting as unverifiable rather than guessing a second
           placement on the same visible line.
