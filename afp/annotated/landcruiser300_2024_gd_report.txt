IMPORTANT METHODOLOGY NOTE (read first)
----------------------------------------
The pipeline's auto-picked reference image (source_images/landcruiser300_2024_gd.png) is
page 1 of the source PDF ("138572 - AFP - LAND CRUISER 300 - GENERAL DUTIES - FITTING.pdf"),
a TEXT-ONLY checklist page with zero embedded images. No vehicle photo exists on that page,
so no tag position could ever be measured against it.

I opened the original PDF (5 pages total) and found:
  - Page 2: a 2x2 clean studio "design mock-up" render (drive side / pass side / front / rear)
  - Page 4: the same mock-up re-rendered darker with the manufacturer's OWN pre-drawn
    reference callouts for the small VC612 lime parts (F1-F5, R1) — used as a cross-check
  - Page 5: REAL installed-vehicle photographs (phone photos, in a warehouse, different
    numberplate "2DN 10F") of drive side, pass side, front bumper, bonnet, and rear — the
    actual "install photos are the bible" page the document itself calls out.

Per the instructions' own priority (actual installed-vehicle photos over a mock-up), I
built my working image entirely from the page-5 REAL photos: I cropped the four panels
(drive / pass / front+bonnet / rear) out of page 5 and reassembled them into one 1521x850
canvas, saved as source_images/landcruiser300_2024_gd_vehicle.png. All coordinates below
are pixel x,y on THAT reassembled image. annotated/landcruiser300_2024_gd_tagged.png is
built on top of it. (Page 4's pre-existing F1-F5/R1 callouts were used only to confirm the
correct numbering order before I independently re-measured each point in the real photo.)

COUNT-MISMATCH FINDING — CONFIRMED TEXT-EXTRACTION ARTIFACT
-------------------------------------------------------------
afp_vehicles.json flags parts_total_stated=216 vs parts_extracted_count=24 for this
vehicle. 216 is almost certainly a parsing artifact — the real PDF has TWO separate
checklists on two different pages, each with its own stated total: page 1 ("PART
CHECKLIST - PRINT PARTS") states "10", and page 3 ("PART CHECKLIST - VC612 LIME PARTS")
states "14". 10 + 14 = 24, which matches parts_extracted_count exactly, so the real
combined total is 24, not 216. There is no sensible reading of the source document that
produces "216" — I could not find a 216 anywhere in either page's text, so I'm confident
this is a corrupted/concatenated digit sequence from automated text extraction, not a real
total. I did not alter the given 17-item list; I just confirm it undercounts physical
quantity (10 decals + 7 line-items representing 14 physical VC612-lime pieces = 17 line
items / 24 physical parts), consistent with parts_extracted_count=24.

KEY VISUAL FINDING: Kings Crest appears placed differently than the checklist implies
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Both DRIVE-2 and PASS-2 are listed as the same "Kings Crest" badge (one per side). I found
it on BOTH sides when I looked at the complete real photo, so no discrepancy after all —
see placements below (I initially missed the pass-side crest and phone-number because my
first measurement crop didn't extend far enough down the pass-side photo; re-checking the
full pass panel located both).

FRONT TRIANGLE: only confirmed on drive side
----------------------------------------------
DRIVE-4 "FRONT TRIANGLE" is a small yellow wedge/chevron decal where the chequer band
meets the front bumper — clearly visible in the real drive-side photo. The real pass-side
photo, however, is framed tighter and simply ends at the front wheel arch, before the
front bumper corner is reached — the corresponding area for PASS-4 is not in the
photograph at all (background warehouse shelving, not vehicle). Per the instructions'
guidance to skip rather than guess, PASS-4 is reported unverifiable below, not placed.
